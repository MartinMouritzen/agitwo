Version 1.1

Fixed
- The Gargoyle at Erasmus' tower was entirely silent: all 28 lines, including the
  three riddles. So were Erasmus' laughs, Baba Yaga's "BE STILL!", the stableman
  and Wolfgang's snoring. 56 clips in total existed but could never play, because
  the voice lookup sat behind a check that skips any line written in capitals.
- The title screen's anti-piracy notice and the read-the-manual notice were voiced
  and kept playing well into the intro. Neither is voiced any more.
- Lines the game fills in as you play could never match a clip, so several answers
  were silent: Erasmus asking about the Open, Fetch, Flame Dart and Trigger spells,
  the Meep's four offers, and all seven thieves' passwords.
- One line listed in the index had no audio file behind it at all.

Changed
- Fenrus recast. The old voice swung between a deep male register and falsetto
  between lines, which read as two different characters in one conversation.
- The Dryad recast, from an androgynous narrator voice to one that suits the
  forest guardian.
- The Warlock's theatrical lines pulled back toward his normal register.
- Erasmus' "Because newts can't talk!" lands as a laugh now rather than a sudden
  falsetto.
- The sheriff's wife's scream and the Hero's snoring are performed rather than read
  out letter by letter.

Added
- macOS (Apple Silicon) and Linux builds.
