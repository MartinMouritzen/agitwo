Version 1.2

Fixed
- Twelve lines were silent because the game assembles them while you play, and one
  of the pieces is the name you typed at character creation, so no fixed recording
  could ever match. Sheriff Schultz's "Good luck in your quest, <your name>." is the
  one every player hits, right after he asks who you are.

  These now speak a generic version of the line, recognised by the parts that never
  change:
    - the Sheriff wishing you luck, and the two other lines that address you by name
    - "You find some silver coins", counting your money, the Thieves' Guild payout
    - the Healer listing what the Dispel Potion still needs
    - the Adventurers' Guild book entries
    - Erasmus grumbling after you beat him at Mage's Maze
    - "Got any of those?" in the tavern card game
